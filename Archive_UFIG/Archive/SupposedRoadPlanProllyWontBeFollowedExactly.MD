## The Council
 - who are they?
  - Not an opposing force, more so an overseer of ArcoSphere production on your current sector, they are neither foe nor friend, they are just the narrative justification for price scaling
 - What can they Control?
  - Mostly the prices of tier 1 and 2 arcospheres
 - Narratively, what do they do?
  - The one who can control or apply tariffs, qoutas, or price reduction and increase for Arcosphere factories and miners
 - GameDesign wise, what do they do?
  - they act as the justification for Price Scaling, they arent exponential, more so Stepping multiplicative, you arent fighting an uphill battle, just big increments, you get side kicked first, but you can recover and get past them
  - they arent just negative and price scaling, they are one of the justification for the event system, once random events is implemented, they can give random bonus or debuffs, its random
